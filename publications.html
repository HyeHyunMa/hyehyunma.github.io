My research centers on:

  • Causal Inference CI : Estimating causal and heterogeneous effects in communication and campaign research.
  • Computational Methods ML BAYES : Applying machine learning, Bayesian modeling, and computational approaches to communication data.
  • Meta-Science MS : Examining statistical power, replicability, evidence quality, and methodological and reporting practices.
  • Public Communication Campaign COMM : Studying health, risk, and public-interest communication, with a focus on campaign strategy and evaluation.
  • Virtual Environments & Social Interaction VR : Investigating user experiences, social interaction, and interpersonal communication in virtual environments.
